About the greenhouse

A local place for gardeners, gift-givers, and flower lovers.

Whether you are planting a full garden, picking up a thoughtful arrangement, or just looking for something bright for the porch, Montrose Greenhouse — His & Herbs offers friendly help and a beautiful variety of plants, flowers, herbs, and garden goods.

The greenhouse is seasonal in selection but open all four seasons, with inventory changing throughout the year for spring planting, summer color, fall décor, winter greenery, and everyday floral needs.

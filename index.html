About the festival

A volunteer-powered tradition with deep roots.

The Montrose Blueberry Festival began with a simple purpose: improve quality of life in the Montrose area, support recreational facilities, and strengthen the community through activities and programs that bring people together.

Today, the festival continues as a symbol of local pride, community volunteerism, and small-town fun.
